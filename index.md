Daily Evolution
One short animated video a day about how animals evolved.

Daily Evolution posts short animated videos on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Each one follows an animal lineage and shows the problem each ancestor faced and the adaptation that solved it.

The "Daily Evolution Uploader" app is the channel's own tool. It uploads the channel's own videos to its own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts and reads those accounts' statistics. It is not offered to other users.

Privacy
Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers the Daily Evolution Uploader app, which uses the YouTube Data API (Google), the Instagram API (Meta) and the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit.

Who uses it: only the owner of the Daily Evolution channel, to manage the channel's own accounts.
What it accesses: permission to upload videos to the channel's own accounts, and read-only access to those accounts' basic profile and statistics.
What it stores: authorization tokens, kept as private secrets on the channel owner's own computer. It stores no data about viewers or other users.
Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties.
Platform terms: use of Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Use of the Instagram API follows the Meta Platform Terms. Use of TikTok's APIs follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.
Revoking access: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram in Settings > Apps and websites, TikTok in Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.
Data deletion: email the contact below and stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.
