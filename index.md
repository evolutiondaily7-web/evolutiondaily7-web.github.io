# Daily Evolution

One short animated video a day about how animals evolved, posted on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

The "Daily Evolution Uploader" app is the channel's own private tool. It uploads the channel's own videos to its own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts and reads those accounts' statistics. It is not offered to other users.

## Privacy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

- Who uses it: only the owner of the Daily Evolution channel, to manage the channel's own accounts.
- What it accesses: permission to upload videos to the channel's own accounts, and read-only access to those accounts' basic profile and statistics.
- What it stores: authorization tokens, kept privately on the owner's own computer. No data about viewers or other users is stored.
- Sharing: no data is sold or shared with anyone.
- Platform rules: Google API use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy (developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy). Instagram use follows the Meta Platform Terms (developers.facebook.com/terms). TikTok use follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.
- Revoking access or deleting data: remove the app in each platform's settings, or email evolutiondaily7@gmail.com and stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.
